# Interface preferences

- Use cobalt blue (`#0047ab`) as the interface accent, with neutral canvas and surfaces. Preserve colors specified by user-provided charts.
- Keep an empty whiteboard blank apart from its functional toolkit. Do not add starter cards, onboarding illustrations, marketing taglines, or empty-state prompts.
- Keep visible copy limited to action labels, meaningful status, errors, and necessary input guidance. Do not add redundant workspace labels, decorative eyebrows, or persistent interaction hints such as “Whiteboard,” “On your whiteboard,” or “Drag to arrange. Right-click to comment.” Keep accessible labels descriptive.
- Open comment and speech-comment editors as small, nonmodal boxes beside their chart. Do not blur, dim, or block the rest of the interface for commenting.
- Keep connection status and pending model-response indicators in the top panel. Use an indeterminate indicator while responses are pending, and clear it on completion, failure, cancellation, or chart deletion.
